# XLIP-010 — Novelty and significance

Formal validity, novelty, and significance are separate statuses.

Statement alignment is a fourth separate status governed by XLIP-021. A
formal proof can be valid while the corresponding informal or scientific claim
is misaligned, weaker than advertised, vacuous, or inconclusive.

Novelty reviewers MUST return evidence roots, corpus cutoff, prior-art coverage, probabilities, confidence, conflicts, and signatures. Weights SHOULD be capped functions of calibration, domain competence, independence, and evidence quality.

The protocol SHOULD preserve minority reports and MUST NOT pay reviewers merely for majority agreement.

A compile-valid but trivial or duplicate result MUST NOT automatically receive novelty or usage rewards.

For open research mining, verified knowledge and rewardable contribution MUST
remain separate decisions. New discovery, first formalization, material proof
improvement, and independently useful replication have distinct eligibility
rules. Immediate commercial demand MUST NOT be required by the open pool.
Renaming, duplication, or artificial partitioning MUST NOT increase the same
contribution's aggregate reward across identities or rounds. Grouping MUST
preserve attribution, state its supporting evidence and uncertainty, and be
appealable; ClaimID inequality alone does not establish novelty. See
[XLIP-024](024-open-research-mining.md) for the normative requirements and
unimplemented allocation, grouping, and appeal services.

Significance estimates are provisional. Mature significance SHOULD be based on observed downstream proof dependencies, verified citations, independent implementations, paid use, compute savings, and successful application evidence.

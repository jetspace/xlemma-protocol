# XLIP-012 — Bounties, grants, and research support

Supported modes are grants, proof bounties, compute pre-purchases, and contractual co-development.

A bounty MUST bind exact claim, theory, trusted challenge, allowed dependencies, axiom policy, required verification policy, reward, deadline, and challenge period. Solver submissions SHOULD use commit-reveal and MAY be encrypted before reveal.

Under the `sponsored_challenge` capsule mode, the sponsor MUST also declare the
contributor allocation, any bounded upstream or impact-pool allocation, result
license, exclusions, and dispute process before work begins. Those economic
terms are prescriptive policy edges; the formal dependency graph cannot add a
new payee after settlement.

Ordinary x402 flows are client-to-service payments. Solver bounties MUST use a distinct escrow or equivalent reverse-direction settlement mechanism.

Negative results, counterexamples, failed proof trajectories, and reusable dead ends MAY receive sponsor or public-goods funding even when no passing proof exists.

# XLIP-002 — Proof Rights Capsule

A Lemma Capsule MUST separate:

- exact formal claim and proof;
- origin certificate;
- human and machine contribution records;
- rights manifest;
- presentations;
- verification and novelty receipts;
- statement-alignment receipts;
- dependencies;
- compute history;
- exactly one economic mode;
- revenue route;
- lineage.

## Origin

Origin certificates are non-transferable signed priority claims. They MUST retain ordering evidence and MUST NOT suppress earlier or competing claims.

## Contributions

Creator-pool shares MUST sum to 10,000 basis points. Amendments MUST reference their parent and preserve prior signatures. Machine assistance MUST be disclosed separately from human contribution.

## Rights

Rights manifests MUST identify controllers, transferability, sublicense terms, jurisdiction where applicable, source-agreement roots, limitations, and employment/university/grant clearance. Token minting MUST NOT expand those rights.

Origin/provenance, artifact/legal rights, and economic participation are three
distinct objects. Economic terms MUST identify a specific payer, revenue
source, calculation base, exclusions, duration, cap, transfer rule, policy
root, and dispute process. A formal dependency MUST NOT create those terms.

## Statement alignment

Formal validity proves an exact formal statement under declared assumptions;
it does not prove that the statement faithfully captures its informal or
scientific interpretation. A `StatementAlignmentReceipt` MUST bind the exact
ClaimID to the informal-claim and presentation hashes, assumptions, reviewed
definitions, domain reviewers, verdict, limitations, and signatures. Formal
and alignment statuses MUST be displayed separately.

## Economic mode

Every capsule selects `commons`, `reciprocal`, `commercial_artifact`, or
`sponsored_challenge` under XLIP-021 and XLIP-022. Commons MUST NOT contain
mandatory per-use fees. Reciprocal, commercial, and sponsored modes require
explicit, bounded, nonrecursive, nonblocking terms.

## Presentation

One claim MAY have many presentations. Presentations MUST reference the exact declaration and SHOULD display a warning that the formal object is authoritative.

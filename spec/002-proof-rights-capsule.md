# XLIP-002 — Proof Rights Capsule

A Lemma Capsule MUST separate:

- exact formal claim and proof;
- origin certificate;
- human and machine contribution records;
- rights manifest;
- presentations;
- verification and novelty receipts;
- dependencies;
- compute history;
- revenue route;
- lineage.

## Origin

Origin certificates are non-transferable signed priority claims. They MUST retain ordering evidence and MUST NOT suppress earlier or competing claims.

## Contributions

Creator-pool shares MUST sum to 10,000 basis points. Amendments MUST reference their parent and preserve prior signatures. Machine assistance MUST be disclosed separately from human contribution.

## Rights

Rights manifests MUST identify controllers, transferability, sublicense terms, jurisdiction where applicable, source-agreement roots, limitations, and employment/university/grant clearance. Token minting MUST NOT expand those rights.

## Presentation

One claim MAY have many presentations. Presentations MUST reference the exact declaration and SHOULD display a warning that the formal object is authoritative.

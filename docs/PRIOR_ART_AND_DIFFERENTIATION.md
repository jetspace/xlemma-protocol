# Prior art and differentiation

xLemma combines several established ideas but gives them a different trust and economic composition.

## Relevant categories

### Formal proof systems

Lean provides a formal language, elaborator, proof objects, and a small trusted kernel. Existing mathematical libraries and tools such as mathlib and leanblueprint provide a strong base for machine-checked mathematics and human-readable project documentation.

### Proof-linked ledgers

Projects such as Proofgold demonstrate that theories, definitions, conjectures, and proofs can be associated with a cryptocurrency network. xLemma does not require a new base blockchain and instead focuses on portable proof objects, independent reproduction receipts, and chain-neutral settlement.

### IP and research tokenization

IP-NFT and decentralized-science systems demonstrate legal/contractual wrappers, research funding, and tokenized participation. xLemma separates attribution, actual legal rights, economic routes, and mathematical truth rather than implying that a token owns a theorem.

### HTTP-native machine payments

x402 provides accountless/machine-oriented payment negotiation over HTTP. xLemma uses it for proof generation, verification, review, storage, and access while keeping the facilitator outside research consensus.

### Decentralized compute markets

Existing compute markets price hardware and tasks. xLemma adds theorem-specific success probability, checker diversity, novelty clearance, and a Verified Proof Cost curve rather than treating raw compute units as the final output.

### Byzantine consensus and sortition

BFT protocols, generalized quorums, and cryptographic sortition provide mechanisms for ordering state and selecting committees. xLemma applies them to evidence production but explicitly refuses to let a majority vote override deterministic checker disagreement.

## xLemma's distinct contribution

1. **Decentralized researcher sovereignty:** one identity, treasury, backed research-credit economy, and proof graph per researcher or collective.
2. **Proof Rights Capsule:** immutable linkage among formal claim, proof, exposition, contribution, machine assistance, rights, verification, compute, and revenue.
3. **Proof of Independent Reproduction:** nodes attest to reproducible observations under explicit implementations and environments rather than casting truth votes.
4. **Generalized checker quorum:** formal certification requires operator, checker-family, provider, and regional diversity with exact root agreement.
5. **Divergence quarantine:** a required independent checker failure cannot be outvoted.
6. **ASTRA/Lean separation:** ASTRA produces and repairs candidates; Lean and independent checkers establish formal acceptance.
7. **Backed researcher credits:** the researcher can pay with their own token without forcing nodes to accept circular self-minted value.
8. **External-revenue compounding:** realized income can automatically create new backed research capacity.
9. **Verified Proof Cost curve:** compares providers and models on cost per accepted, novelty-cleared result rather than unit price alone.
10. **Compute-savings dividend:** useful upstream lemmas can earn from conservatively measured reductions in downstream proof cost, capped by actual revenue.
11. **Open-proof monetization:** creators can earn from services, certified delivery, implementation rights, compute savings, grants, and reputation without privatizing mathematical truth.
12. **Portable architecture:** existing chains, model providers, storage systems, and checkers are adapters rather than permanent sovereign dependencies.

## What xLemma deliberately is not

- not a token vote on mathematical truth;
- not a claim that formulas themselves are automatically owned as property;
- not one speculative token per theorem;
- not an ASTRA self-verification service;
- not an uncapped recursive royalty graph;
- not a replacement for peer review, novelty analysis, legal rights clearance, or scientific replication;
- not a production-ready financial or smart-contract system in this reference release.

## Defensibility

The likely long-term defensibility is not the token. It is the integrated data and trust graph:

- canonical formal claims and proof objects;
- independently reproducible checker evidence;
- calibrated ASTRA/prover trajectories;
- point-in-time proof-cost curves;
- high-quality novelty and equivalence graph;
- contribution and rights provenance;
- verified downstream reuse and compute-savings measurements;
- reputation based on delayed calibration and reproducibility.

That graph can improve routing, cost estimation, theorem search, funding allocation, and model training while remaining inspectable and portable.
